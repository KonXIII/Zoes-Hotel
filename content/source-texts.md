## 1. Welcome / About

Dear future guests!

Zoes is a family-run hotel, located on the outskirts of Faliraki Resort, in Ladiko area, surrounded by beautiful gardens & a large swimming pool with a bar and a restaurant.
The marvelous Ladiko Beach and the unique Anthony Quinn Bay can be reached within 15 minutes on foot!

The Hotel offers 48 modern comfortable Superior Rooms, with garden, mountain, sea and pool views, that can accommodate 2, or 3 persons & 10 Suites, with swim up access to a private pool, ideal to accommodate couples or 2 friends (adults only over 21 years old).

We are close to a number of attractions and places of interest, such as the Kallithea Therme, the Rhodes Old town & Grand Master Palace, the Aquarium, the Water Park, the Casino, the Afandou Golf course, Water Sports & more.

Zoes restaurant serves all day traditional Greek dishes and delicious international cuisine!
Our Hotel is an excellent value for money 3 Star complex, perfect for all types of travelers & a very popular choice for those seeking a lively and a family friendly atmosphere, and also a good base from which they can enjoy the nightlife in Faliraki, and explore the superb surroundings of Rhodes Island!

We are looking forward to welcoming you in our Hotel! Zoes Hotel offers 48 modern and comfortable rooms, that can accommodate 2, 3, or 4 people & 10 Suites, with swim up access to a private pool, ideal to accommodate couples or 2 friends (adults only over 21 years old)

There is a 200 sq. m. Swimming pool for adults, sun terrace with sun beds and umbrellas (free of charge), a swimming pool for children, children's playground, pool table, and Free Wi Fi internet access all around the Hotel (rooms also)

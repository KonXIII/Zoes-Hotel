## 1. The business
- **What they do (one sentence):** A family-run, 3-star hotel in Ladiko, a few minutes from Faliraki on Rhodes. It has 48 rooms, 10 adults-only swim-up suites and a big garden pool. (site)
- **What they sell / the offer:** 48 **Superior Rooms**: 32 m², one double + 2 sofa beds, **3 adults, or 2 adults + 2 children** (owner, 2026-11-06), furnished balcony with garden/mountain/pool/sea views. 10 **Swim-Up Suites**: 30 m² + 13 m² veranda with 2 loungers, king bed + sofa single bed, 45" Smart TV, swim-up access to a private pool, **max 2 guests, adults 21+ only, no children**. Also a buffet breakfast, the all-day Zoes restaurant (Greek and international), a pool bar and snack bar, and a mini market. Full facts: `content/source-texts.md`.
- **Stage / context:** Established hotel, website rebuild. The current site is an older template with no separate rooms or facilities pages and TripAdvisor reviews from 2022.

## 2. The audience

(2) **Couples / adults** wanting the 10 adults-only (21+) swim-up suites. One warm hotel voice with clearly separate routes ("Family rooms" / "Adults-only suites").

## 4. Positioning & competitors
- **Top 2–3 competitors:** **Kresten Palace** (4★, Kallithea, krestenpalace.gr), the only nearby hotel selling both swim-up rooms *and* family rooms, also on reserve-online.net, with a strong "Why book direct" block. **Lymberia** (3★ Faliraki, lymberia.com), with swim-up rooms (shared pool) and a kids' pool, shows prices (swim-up from about $150), but has no booking engine. **Lime Hotel** (new 2025, 45 rooms, limehotel.gr), cheap and family-focused, with a "best price for direct" promise. Swim-up benchmark only: **Cabú** (5★ SLH adults-only, cabu.gr). Story competitor: **Ladiko Inn** (family-run, 20 rooms, ladikoinn.com).
- **What they do well / where they're weak:** Kresten does book-direct best but looks generic. Cabú has polished serif craft and 6 languages, but no reason to book direct and no prices. Ladiko Inn has a warm, named family story ("Billy, Debby, Marietta, and Panormitis", "250 m from Anthony Quinn Bay") on a thin Wix page. Lymberia, Lime and Olympia Sun have weak, broken or missing sites. **No one shows review scores clearly on-site.** Only Kresten and Lime state a book-direct perk.
- **Our single differentiator (the wedge):** **Two holidays in one family-run hotel.** A garden-and-pool family hotel (48 rooms for up to 3 adults or 2+2) and, in its own wing, 10 adults-only (21+) swim-up suites, all a short walk from Ladiko beach and Anthony Quinn Bay. No nearby competitor frames it this way, and none runs a 21+ wing inside a family hotel (Cabú lists 16+). Back it with: clear reviews (9+/10), real photos, and concrete book-direct reasons.
- **Proof we can back it up (data, cases, credentials):** The hotel has TripAdvisor and Booking.com ratings and reviews, which the **owner will supply later**. Build a reviews/score slot fed from `content/` and launch with real numbers only, no placeholders.

## 5. Constraints
- **Non-negotiables (brand, legal, must-include):** Logo: two versions in `Images/logos/`: `current ZOES Logo.png` (yellow cartoon sun + blue script) and `new logo.svg` (navy sunburst, high-contrast serif "Zoe's", copper "HOTEL & SUITES"). **Decision 2026-11-06: use `new logo.svg`.** The logo keeps its own navy + copper. The site UI palette stays warm (limestone + teal), with no navy UI. Avoid a navy / "premium corporate" palette, which the owner rejected in May 2026. Launch languages: **English + Greek** (EN default; EL as a full translation, with hreflang). NSRF/ESPA EU co-funding banner: owner doesn't know if it's required. Leave out for now and keep a footer slot ready. (The old banner file was in the removed `brand/` folder.) Ask the accountant/consultant before launch.
- **Timeline / launch date:** No fixed date (owner, 2026-11-06). Sensible target: live before 2027 booking season opens (early spring).
- **Who edits content after launch:** Undecided. Maybe a CMS later, but **not TinaCMS**. The owner may pick a different CMS. Build content-driven: all copy, rooms, facilities, FAQ and photos live in typed data files (`content/`), so any headless CMS can be wired in later without touching components.

## 6. Success definition
- **We'll know this worked if…** By the end of the 2027 season the direct share of reservations (engine vs OTAs) is clearly up on 2026. The site also ranks #1 for "Zoe's Hotel Faliraki" ahead of the OTAs, and guests stop calling to ask what's already on the site (FAQ, distances, policies).

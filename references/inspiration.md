## Editorial / type-led
- https://www.arcmovement.com — hand-feel, serif, editorial rhythm
- https://artspaceworks.com — restrained typography, honest photography

## Product / SaaS
- https://linear.app — marketing site craft level, motion discipline
- https://vercel.com — dark-mode done right, type hierarchy

## Studio / agency
- https://www.instrument.com — case study pacing
- https://resn.co.nz — motion + wow moments, use sparingly

## Luxury / brand
- https://aesop.com — generous whitespace, zero decoration
- https://offf.com — maximalist type done seriously

## Motion / experimental
- https://igloo.inc — scroll choreography references
- https://lusion.co — WebGL as structure, not decoration

## Anti-references (what NOT to look like)
- Most YC company pages circa 2024 — purple gradient + Inter + cyan accent.
- Generic "SaaS dashboard" hero with floating cards at -15° rotation.
- Stock hero illustration of 3 diverse people around a laptop.

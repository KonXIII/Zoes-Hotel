## Users
- **Primary ✅**: Two groups served equally, each with its own path. **Families** booking a summer week on Rhodes (Superior Rooms: 3 adults or 2 + 2 kids, kids' pool, playground, an easy beach). **Couples / adult friends** booking one of the 10 adults-only (21+) Swim-Up Suites. Mostly on **mobile**, often comparing against Booking.com in another tab.
- **Secondary**: Returning guests checking dates, transfers and the restaurant. Greek-speaking visitors (full EL version).
- **Emotional ask**: Real warmth and an honest picture ("this is exactly what you get"). The reassurance that booking direct is safe and simple.

## Brand personality *(proposed, anchored to the owner's own texts)*
- Hospitable: "Dear future guests!" is the house voice, and we keep its warmth without the exclamation marks.
- Family-run, honest: a 3-star that is proud of its value, never posing as a 5-star.
- Sunny, unhurried: the garden, the pool and the walk to Ladiko beach.
- Practical: distances, times and prices stated plainly.

## Aesthetic direction
- **Style ✅**: Warm editorial. A magazine-like serif for headings, big honest photographs, a calm pace and a personal family voice. Boutique without pretending.
- **References I love**: aurelia-template.framer.website (owner, 2026-11-06). See `references/sites/aurelia-template.framer.website/DESIGN.md`. More may follow.
- **Anti-references** *(proposed)*: Navy / "premium corporate" hotel templates (owner rejected these in May 2026). OTA-style listing pages crammed with icons and badges. Glossy 5-star resort sites with dark glamour and gold. Booking-widget-on-top-of-carousel templates.
- **Dark / light**: Light-first. Night photos are used as moments, not as the theme.
- **Density**: Generous whitespace for the story, then dense and scannable for facts (amenities, distances, FAQ).

## Color direction ✅ OWNER PALETTE (2026-11-06, final for now): Salt flat white #EBECEE · Rift valley blue #264A75 · Obsidian rock #262626 · Desert heat #C28B5B
- Page #F3F4F5, surfaces #EBECEE, text obsidian, muted #5C5F64. Booking buttons = desert heat with OBSIDIAN text (white fails, 2.9:1). Links/icons = rift blue. Dark band = rift blue, small text on it #E2B68D. Light footer. Tokens: app/globals.css @theme.
- **Logo: the OFFICIAL logo** (`public/logo-official.png`: yellow sun + blue "Zoe's" script, as on the sign and key cards), always used unchanged. `Images/logos/new logo.svg` was only the owner's concept and is not used.
- **Nav: frosted "liquid glass" bar** (owner's Nutrify material), fixed and the same on every page, no scroll states.

### (superseded) Palette A "Olive grove & stone" (owner, 2026-11-06; replaces limestone + teal/copper, which "shouts Claude")
- Sampled from the hotel's photos. Stone `#F3F1EB` / `#E6E3D8`, ink `#22261E`, muted `#5E6156`, olive accent `#4E5A33` (booking, links, icons), grove dark band `#2F3424`, gold highlight `#C9A869` (on dark only). Tokens in `app/globals.css` @theme.

### (superseded) Limestone + teal
- **Hue**: Warm limestone / sand base (the white render and stone wall). Oak-brown ink for text (the slatted headboards). **The rooms' teal** (cushions, pool) is the single accent, used for booking actions and small highlights.
- **Saturation**: Muted base, with one saturated accent (teal) used sparingly.
- **Contrast**: Medium-high. Body text on limestone must clear WCAG AA. Teal buttons need AA text contrast, which probably means a deeper teal for fills.
- **Booking accent changed 2026-11-06 (owner: the teal CTA looked "terrible")**: booking buttons are now **copper `#A85E25`**, from the logo's sunburst, deepened for AA. Teal stays for links, icons and small highlights only.
- **Hard rule**: No navy or dark blue in the UI. The logo's navy + copper stay **inside the logo only**. Copper may appear as a tiny secondary at most.

## Typography ✅ (owner's pick on /fonts, 2026-11-06)
- **Display: Noto Serif Display** (variable, italic, full Greek), weight 450 (400 ran thin in Greek), tracking -0.02em.
- **Text: Manrope** (variable, full Greek), 15–18px.
- Replaced EB Garamond + Commissioner, which read bookish next to the official logo and the new palette.

## Motion temperament *(proposed)*
- **Overall**: Restrained.
- **Scroll behavior**: Smooth (Lenis), light. Kit-default blur-in titles and gentle reveals.
- **Allowed**: Micro-interactions and one hero flourish (e.g. the day → dusk pool moment). No scroll-jacking and no parallax on text.

## Design principles *(proposed)*
1. **Photographs tell the truth.** Real Zoe's photos only, shown big and uncropped where it matters. Never stock.
2. **Two doors, one house.** Families and couples each get a clear path (Superior Rooms / Swim-Up Suites), told in the same warm voice.
3. **The booking button is always one tap away**, and the site says why booking direct is better.
4. **Facts are plain.** Distances, times, policies and capacities are stated exactly as true, never inflated.

## Non-negotiables
- WCAG AA contrast site-wide.
- Touch targets ≥ 44×44px.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
- No pure black/white. Tint neutrals toward limestone.
- No Inter/Roboto/Arial as default body.
- Every font must render Greek.
- No navy/dark-blue UI (see the memory note on the corporate palette).
